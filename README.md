## Flareless Torches

![latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/taipan9129/flareless-torches)
![commit count](https://img.shields.io/github/commit-activity/t/taipan9129/flareless-torches)

Ever needed a blank slate for your fancy custom flares? Well then you're in luck because this simple plugin adds flareless variants for the following:
- Z-333 and Z-666 (can be found anywhere fusion torches are sold)
- Korath afterburners (available in Laki Nemparu)
- Remnant thrusters
